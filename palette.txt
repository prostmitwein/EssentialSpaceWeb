==============================================================================
              ESSENTIAL SPACE WEB — COLOR PALETTE SPECIFICATION
              Based on Nothing OS 5 Design Principles (Grey & Off-Tones)
              + Project-Specific Brand Accent Colors
==============================================================================

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
Core palette follows Nothing OS 5 focus on grey and off-tone translucent 
overlays rather than solid colors. Yellow and Red are PROJECT-SPECIFIC 
accent colors added for this application (not part of Nothing OS official).

==============================================================================
